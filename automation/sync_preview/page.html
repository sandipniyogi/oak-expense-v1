Conflict test

This deliberately bypasses the rehearsal edit gate to simulate independent edits made before a lock was installed. Regular edits above remain gated.

How to test

  1. Edit a record on one side and save. Further edits lock.
  2. Click Check & Sync. Both stores should match.
  3. Save on the other side, then sync again.
  4. Switch a peer offline or its approval off. Normal saves and sync must fail.
  5. Restore both switches. Try an interrupted sync, then recover.
  6. Inject a conflict. Sync must refuse to overwrite it; select a version and sync.

The switches simulate connectivity and approval. They do not check real network connectivity or GitHub.
